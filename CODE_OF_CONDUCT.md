# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and protect other people's
privacy. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention,
doxxing, and abuse of project infrastructure are not tolerated.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the
community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security
contact.

This policy is inspired by Contributor Covenant 2.1.
