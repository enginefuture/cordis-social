# Acceptable use

Cordis Social is intended for authorized account management, personal posting,
customer-support workflows, accessibility, QA, and internal operations.

Do not use it for credential attacks, CAPTCHA bypass, fake engagement, spam,
bulk account creation, platform manipulation, harassment, or automation against
accounts and services you do not own or have permission to operate.

Platform terms, API terms, rate limits, and applicable laws still apply. The
project may decline support for workflows that conflict with this policy.
